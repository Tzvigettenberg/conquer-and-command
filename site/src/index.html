Conquer & Command is a homage to Command & Conquer Generals: Zero Hour with a budget of exactly nothing: procedural low-poly models, an authoritative server sim at 20 Hz, generals' promotions, battle plans, superweapons, garrisonable villages and three full factions with their own bots.

Host a game with a room name (and a password if you like), your friends pick it from the open-games list, and everyone chooses a side in the lobby. Any slot can be a human, an Easy / Medium / Hard general, or left open — and anyone can step out and observe the whole map with a live scoreboard.

FACTIONS

USA Technology

Expensive, precise and airborne. Rangers, Missile Defenders, Pathfinders and Colonel Burton on foot; Humvees, Crusaders, Paladins, Tomahawks and Avengers on the ground; Comanches, Raptors, Stealth Fighters and the untouchable Aurora overhead. Every vehicle can carry one escort drone.

Powers Spy Drone, A-10 Warthog strike, Spectre Gunship, Fuel Air Bomb · Superweapon Particle Cannon — steer the beam with clicks while it burns.

China Numbers & firepower

Red Guards come in pairs, Hackers earn cash while idle, Black Lotus captures from a distance. Battlemasters, Gattling Tanks, Dragon flame tanks, Overlords and Nuke Cannons roll out of the War Factory; MiGs and Helixes from the Airfield. Five heavies together earn the horde bonus.

Powers Cash Hack, Artillery Barrage, Frenzy, Carpet Bomb · Superweapon Nuclear Missile, and the fallout lingers.

GLA No power needed

Workers build and haul. Rebels, RPG Troopers, Terrorists and Jarmen Kell; Technicals, Scorpions, Quad Cannons, Rocket Buggies, Toxin Tractors, Marauders and Bomb Trucks. The Tunnel Network moves your army across the map, and Demo Traps wait for whoever follows.

Powers Rebel Ambush, Cash Bounty, Anthrax Bomb, Sneak Attack · Superweapon SCUD Storm — nine toxin warheads every four minutes.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS

Everyone downloads the same build

Unzip and run ConquerAndCommand.exe. Windows may warn about an unknown publisher the first time — More info → Run anyway. The host refuses players on a different version, so grab the newest zip whenever there's an update.

One person hosts

Press Host game. On the same Wi-Fi, friends just join your local IP. Over the internet, either forward UDP port 7788 on your router and share your public IP, or everyone installs a free mesh VPN such as Tailscale or ZeroTier and uses that IP — no router setup at all.

Set up the lobby and go

Pick a map (2 to 6 spawn slots), a faction per slot, teams, starting cash, superweapons on or off. Fill empty slots with AI generals or close them, or press Observe to watch. When everyone's in, Start.

Playing alone? Host and press Start solo (sandbox), or set every slot to AI and hit Watch the AIs fight. Two copies on one PC work too: host in one, join 127.0.0.1 in the other.

CONTROLS

The Generals layout, so your hands already know it.

Left-dragSelect — double-click grabs every unit of that type on screen, Shift adds
Right-clickMove, attack, repair, gather, capture, board — Shift queues
A + clickAttack-move — works on the minimap too
Q / W / ESelect all combat units / aircraft / same type on screen (tap twice for the whole map)
GGuard an area — drag to size the circle
S · XStop · scatter (or force-fire with a target)
N · Ctrl+UpNext Dozer / Worker · next builder
B F I C YJump to Barracks / War Factory / Airfield / Command Center / Supply Center
Ctrl+1…9Control groups — press twice to jump there
H · SpaceHome base · last attack alert
[ ] · wheelRotate · zoom (middle-drag tilts, numpad 5 resets)
EnterChat with everyone (Backspace: allies only) · Ctrl+B drops a beacon
